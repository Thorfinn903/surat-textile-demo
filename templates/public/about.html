Our Journey

2005

The Beginning — IT WORLD

Started as a small computer repair shop named "IT WORLD" in Surat's bustling Millennium Market. A dream of entrepreneurship with just ₹50,000 capital.

2010

The Pivot — Entering Textiles

Noticed the massive opportunity in Surat's textile industry. Pivoted from IT to textile trading, starting with Saree manufacturing and wholesale supply.

2015

Scaling Up — 100+ Retailers

Built a network of 100+ retail partners across Gujarat and Maharashtra. Established our own quality checking process every piece inspected before dispatch.

2020

Going Digital — WhatsApp Business

Adopted WhatsApp Business for catalog sharing and instant orders. During COVID, this became our lifeline 3x growth while traditional shops struggled.

2024

Digital Dukan — The SaaS Platform

Launched Digital Dukan — a complete digital storefront with real-time catalog, WhatsApp integration, and analytics. Now serving 500+ retailers nationwide.
